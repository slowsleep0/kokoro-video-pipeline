## The Hidden Ocean

The ocean covers more than seventy percent of our planet, yet we have explored only a tiny fraction of it.

Every day, its currents move vast amounts of water, heat, and nutrients around the globe, quietly shaping the weather above our heads.

## Life in Darkness [dark]

Below the surface, sunlight fades within the first two hundred meters. Beyond that point, life continues in complete darkness, powered by chemical energy instead of the sun.

Scientists estimate that millions of marine species are still undiscovered, waiting in deep trenches, colorful reefs, and the open water.

## One Blue Planet

The ocean is not just scenery. It produces much of the oxygen we breathe, feeds billions of people, and stores heat that would otherwise warm the land even faster. [fade]

Protecting it may be the single most important thing our generation can do for the next one. [slide: up]
